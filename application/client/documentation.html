Platform Manual

Procedural standards and best practices for sales representatives processing high-end jewelry transactions.

1. System Summary

The Fine Jewelry Sales Management Platform offers a protected, unified online hub crafted for sales teams across our retail network. It streamlines rapid order registration for high-value items, handles automated tax computations on the server, and ensures robust transaction tracking.

2. User Procedures

Following successful login, sales representatives reach the main navigation hub, featuring two core workflows:

a) Register New Order

Allows representatives to log customer purchases. Upon inputting client contact information, staff choose bands, chains, or fine timepieces from the master product line and define item counts. The server-side script calculates the final amount including 21% sales tax and records the purchase.

b) Browse Order History

Grants access to lookup features to review the master database of store sales via a structured table layout or query individual transactions by their unique identifier.
